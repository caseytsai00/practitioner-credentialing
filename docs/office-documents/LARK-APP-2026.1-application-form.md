# **Application for initial appointment and clinical privileges — blank form**

Larkhollow Community Hospital · Medical Staff Office Form version **LARK-APP-2026.1**. This is the blank form. A completed form becomes revision 1 of an application; a correction becomes revision 2, and so on. **A revision never replaces the one before it in the file.**

Complete every section. An incomplete application is returned with a written list of what is missing, and the reply is due within 30 calendar days of the date on that letter.

## Section A — Identity and contact

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Field | What to enter |
| Last name, First name, Middle name | As shown on your licence. Leave middle name blank only if you have none |
| Birth year | Four digits |
| Degree | MD or DO |
| National Provider Identifier | Ten digits, if you hold one. **This is an identifier for billing and is not a licence.** Entering it does not declare a licence |
| Practice state | The two-letter state in which you will see patients under this appointment |
| Position sought | The role this appointment is for |
| Clinical discipline | The discipline you practise, chosen from: Family Medicine, Internal Medicine, Hospital Medicine, Cardiovascular Disease, Emergency Medicine, General Surgery, Obstetrics and Gynecology, Pediatrics, Anesthesiology, Diagnostic Radiology, Psychiatry, Orthopaedic Surgery. **Your peer referees must practise the discipline you name here** |

## Section B — Authorization and release

I authorize Larkhollow Community Hospital to request and receive information about me from any school, training programme, licensing board, certifying board, employer, affiliated institution and professional reference named in this application, and I authorize each of them to release it. I release each of them from liability for doing so.

Signature and date. **Until this is signed and dated, the office sends no verification request of any kind**, and the application cannot advance.

## Section C — Privileges requested

List every privilege group you request, by the code and name published in the current privilege criteria. Name the criteria version you are requesting under. At least one group is required.

## Section D — Education and post-graduate training

One row per entry. Medical school first, then every internship, residency, fellowship or other organized professional training, in date order.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Field | What to enter |
| Entry type | medical-school, residency, fellowship, or other |
| Organization, Location | The school or sponsoring institution, and its town and state |
| Role | Degree sought, or the programme and position |
| From date, To date | YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive |
| Verification contact name and email | Whom the office should write to |

## Section E — Employment, affiliations, teaching and military service

One row per entry, in date order, running continuously from the day after you finished professional school to today.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Field | What to enter |
| Entry type | employment, affiliation, teaching, military, or other |
| Organization, Location | The employer or institution, and its town and state |
| Role | Your position |
| From date, To date | YYYY-MM-DD, inclusive. **Leave To date blank only if the entry is current** |
| Hours per week | Whole hours. A part-time entry is still an entry |
| Verification contact name and email | Whom the office should write to |
| Notes | Anything the dates alone do not explain |

**Overlapping entries are expected** — a part-time clinic post alongside a hospital appointment is two entries, not an error. Enter both.

## Section F — Explanation of periods not covered

For every period of **30 days or more** since you finished professional school that no entry in Section D or E covers, attach a written explanation. One explanation per period. **Begin the explanation with the line Period explained: <from date> to <to date>**, and make sure that period covers the whole of the period you are explaining — the office matches your explanation to the period by those two dates and by nothing else.

The office records your explanation. **The office does not judge it.** Whether an explanation is satisfactory is the Clinical Director's decision.

An application with a period of 30 days or more and no written explanation for it is returned as incomplete.

## Section G — Licences and certifications

One row per credential, **including every licence and certification you have ever held, active or inactive**, in any state and from any board.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Field | What to enter |
| Class | licence or certification |
| Issuer | The state board or the certifying board |
| Jurisdiction or board | Two-letter state for a licence; the board's short code for a certification |
| Number | The credential number |
| Status | Your own description: Active, Inactive, Expired, Board certified, Board eligible |
| Issue date, Expiry date | YYYY-MM-DD |

What you enter here is your declaration. The office verifies each entry with the issuing board, and the board's answer is what goes in the file.

## Section H — Peer references

Name at least **two** peer referees. For each: name, credential, organization, specialty, working address, and how you have worked together.

A referee must have worked directly with you within the past two years, must practise in the same clinical discipline, and must not be a relative, spouse or partner. The office writes to each referee directly; a reference you carry to us yourself cannot be used.

## Section I — Professional practice questions

Answer every question Yes or No. **Every Yes requires a written explanation in the comment box beside it.** Leaving a question unanswered makes the application incomplete.

|  |  |
| :-: | :-: |
| Code | Question |
| PPQ-1 | Has any licence or registration you hold or have held ever been denied, suspended, revoked, restricted, placed on probation, made subject to a monitoring agreement, surrendered, or subjected to any other action by a licensing board? |
| PPQ-2 | Have your clinical privileges at any institution ever been denied, reduced, restricted, suspended, revoked, not renewed, or made subject to conditions? |
| PPQ-3 | Have you ever resigned from, or withdrawn an application to, a medical staff or training programme while an investigation was pending or in exchange for not conducting an investigation? |
| PPQ-4 | Has any board certification you hold or have held ever been revoked, suspended, or not renewed for cause? |
| PPQ-5 | Have you ever been the subject of a professional review action reportable to a national data bank? |
| PPQ-6 | Have you ever been excluded, debarred or suspended from participation in a federal or state health care programme? |
| PPQ-7 | Have you ever been convicted of, or pleaded guilty or no contest to, a criminal offence other than a minor traffic violation? |
| PPQ-8 | Is there any physical or mental condition, or any use of a substance, that currently impairs your ability to perform the privileges you are requesting, with or without accommodation? |

A Yes to any of these is a high-risk finding. It is not by itself a bar to appointment. It is routed to the Clinical Director and presented to the Executive Committee of the Medical Staff and to the Governing Body with the evidence.

If a verification the office receives shows that a Yes was owed and a No was given, **the office will contact you to confirm the answer** before the file goes any further.

## Section J — Attestation

I attest that everything in this application is complete, current and correct. I understand that the burden of supplying verifiable information is mine, and that a discrepancy between what I have declared and what a source reports will be put back to me in writing.

Signature and date.

### What the office does with a completed form

The office records receipt, screens the nine completeness conditions in the procedure, and either takes the file up or writes to you with the missing items. It then writes to the schools, programmes, boards, employers, affiliations and referees named above. It does not use anything you hand over yourself in place of their replies.
