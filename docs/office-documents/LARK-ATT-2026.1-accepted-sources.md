# Accepted sources by element — who may attest

Larkhollow Community Hospital · Medical Staff Office Table version **LARK-ATT-2026.1**, in force
from 2026-01-01. Maintained by the Medical Services Professional.

The manual we adopted leaves its source lists open. Section 6, Subsection 4, element 2 ends
"etc., as long as the source is a designated equivalent source for education and training
verifications", and element 5 ends "or other nationally recognized certifying body, as long as the
source is a designated equivalent source for board certification verifications".

**Larkhollow rule.** An open list cannot be applied the same way twice, so ours is closed. This
table is the whole list. A reply from a source on it is admissible. **A reply from any source not
on it is inadmissible and is raised to the Medical Services Professional** — it is not quietly
accepted, and it is not quietly treated as a secondary source.

Two rules cut across every row, both the manual's, both from Section 6, Subsection 1:
"Primary source verifications received directly from the applicant cannot be used."
"Copies of a diploma, license, registration, etc., in place of verifications are unacceptable."
So the applicant is never an accepted source for any element, on any row below, however genuine
the document they carry. The reply is filed as received and the request stays open.

## Education and training

| Class | Accepted sources |
|---|---|
| Primary source | The degree-granting school's registrar's office or dean's office. The sponsoring institution's graduate medical education office or programme director, for a residency or fellowship |
| Designated equivalent source | Educational Commission for Foreign Medical Graduates (ECFMG). Federation Credentials Verification Service of the Federation of State Medical Boards (FCVS). American Medical Association Physician Profile. American Osteopathic Association Physician Profile. National Student Clearinghouse |
| Secondary source — **only on impossibility** | The applicant's most recent affiliation's medical staff office, stating how it obtained its own verification, when the school or programme has closed and no designated equivalent source holds the record. The reason is written into the file |
| Not accepted | The applicant. An alumni association. A departmental colleague writing in a personal capacity. A recruiter, staffing agency or locum tenens agency. A photocopied or scanned certificate from any hand |

## Licensure

| Class | Accepted sources |
|---|---|
| Primary source | The issuing state licensing board, through its own online verification service or a written reply from the board |
| Designated equivalent source | Federation of State Medical Boards Physician Data Center |
| Secondary source | None. A state licensing board does not close |
| Not accepted | The applicant. A prior employer's copy of a licence. An aggregator or directory not named above |

## Board certification

| Class | Accepted sources |
|---|---|
| Primary source | The issuing certifying board, through its own verification service or a written reply |
| Designated equivalent source | American Board of Medical Specialties (ABMS) certification verification. CertiFACTS. American Medical Association Physician Profile. American Osteopathic Association Physician Profile. American Board of Physician Specialties (ABPS). American Nurses Credentialing Center (ANCC). National Commission on Certification of Physician Assistants (NCCPA) |
| Secondary source | None |
| Not accepted | The applicant. A certificate image. A specialty society membership roster |

## Affiliations and employment

| Class | Accepted sources |
|---|---|
| Primary source | The affiliated hospital's medical staff office. The employer's human resources department or the practice's authorized administrator |
| Designated equivalent source | A credentials verification organization retained by that affiliation or employer, where the reply names the organization and the affiliation it acted for |
| Secondary source — **only on impossibility** | Where the employer has dissolved and no successor holds the records, a written account from the applicant's most recent surviving affiliation of what that affiliation verified, with the reason written into the file |
| Not accepted | The applicant. A supervisor writing from a personal address without the employer's authority. A professional networking profile. A letter of recommendation offered in place of a dates-and-position verification |

## What to do with a reply that is not on this table

1. Record it as received, with who sent it and how it arrived. Nothing is discarded.
2. Mark the entry still outstanding. An inadmissible reply does not resolve anything.
3. Keep requesting from a source that is on the table, and keep logging each attempt.
4. Raise it to the Medical Services Professional, who decides whether the case is one of the
   impossibility routes above.

**Non-response is not impossibility.** A source that exists and does not answer is a
non-responsive source: it stays outstanding, the attempts keep being logged, and the manual's red
flag applies. Only a source that no longer exists opens the secondary route.
