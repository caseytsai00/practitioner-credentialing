# **Reserved authority — who holds what**

Larkhollow Community Hospital · Medical Staff Office Roster version **LARK-AUTH-2026.1**, effective 2026-01-01. Maintained by the Medical Staff Office from the Governing Body's appointment records.

**Every decision record is validated against this roster.** A record that names a signatory and a role is checked here: did that person hold that role on the decision date, and may the body they signed for record that outcome. A record's own claim of authority is not evidence of it.

All names below are fictional.

## **Who may record what**

|  |  |  |
| :-: | :-: | :-: |
| Body | May record | May not record |
| Credentials Committee | recommended, not recommended, deferred-pending-information | any approval |
| Executive Committee of the Medical Staff | recommended, not recommended, deferred-pending-information | any approval |
| Governing Body | approved, approved-with-conditions, deferred-pending-information, denied | — |

The manual we adopted puts it in one sentence, Section 12: "All signatories should utilize a "recommend" selection, with only the Chair of the GB or their designee using the "approval" selection."

A record from the Credentials Committee or the Executive Committee of the Medical Staff that carries the word approved is still not an approval. Those bodies cannot approve. Nothing activates on it.
