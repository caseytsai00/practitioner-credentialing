# **Privilege criteria — initial appointment**

Larkhollow Community Hospital · Medical Staff Office Criteria version **LARK-PRIV-2026.1**, in force from 2026-01-01. Set by the Clinical Director with the Executive Committee of the Medical Staff. Published and applied by the Medical Staff Office. The office applies these criteria; the office does not write them and does not extend them for an individual applicant.

**Every decision record cites the criteria version it was decided under.** A file decided under one version is not re-read under another; a new version re-opens the mapping for files not yet decided.

The manual we adopted says what criteria must cover, in Section 9: "At a minimum, privilege criteria should address the required education level, formal training requirements, and current experience." The three columns below are those three.

## **How the office applies the table**

1. **A requested group is supported** when the applicant holds a verified certification from one of the boards named in that group's row, in the specialty named, with certification_status Active on the decision date, **and** the verified training meets the minimum training requirement.
2. **A requested group is unsupported** when neither condition holds. The finding names that group. Other requested groups are unaffected.
3. **An inactive or expired certification does not support a privilege group.** It is still verified and still recorded — what the applicant is certified in and what the applicant was once certified in are different facts — but it supports nothing.
4. **Board eligible** is accepted in place of an active certification for PRIV-FM, PRIV-IM, PRIV-HOSP and PRIV-PED only, and only when the issuing board confirms in writing that the applicant is a candidate in good standing and states the date the eligibility ends, and that date is in the future on the decision date. Board eligible is **not** accepted for PRIV-CARD, PRIV-EM, PRIV-GS, PRIV-OBG, PRIV-ANES, PRIV-RAD, PRIV-PSY or PRIV-ORTHO.
5. **PRIV-CARD requires both**: the cardiovascular disease fellowship and the internal medicine residency that precedes it. A cardiology certification without the residency, or the residency without the fellowship, does not support it.
6. **When nothing the applicant holds supports any requested group**, this is a threshold eligibility question. The office raises it to the Clinical Director, who decides whether the application is discontinued. The office does not discontinue an application. The office raises it on what has been verified at the export date: while nothing yet verified supports any requested group, the question stands with the Clinical Director, and it is withdrawn without a disposition when a later verification supports the request.
7. **The current-experience column is not the office's to apply.** Current competency is determined by clinical leadership, not the office (the manual, Section 6, Subsection 4, element 8), and it is one of the elements this procedure does not handle. Whether a group is supported is decided by rules 1 to 6 alone; the current-experience requirement travels with the packet for the committee and never makes a group unsupported.

Whether a supported request should be granted is not settled here. That is the Executive Committee of the Medical Staff's recommendation and the Governing Body's decision.
