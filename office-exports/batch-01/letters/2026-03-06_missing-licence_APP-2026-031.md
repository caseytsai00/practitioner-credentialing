**Date:** 2026-03-06

**Application:** APP-2026-031

Thank you for your note of 2026-02-20. A National Provider Identifier is an identifier for billing; it is not a licence and it says nothing about whether anybody is licensed. Your Oregon licence is current and we have verified it, and we will verify every other licence you hold, but Larkhollow's bylaws require one current, active, full and unrestricted licence issued by the State of Washington, which is our practice state. The standard we work to is more generous than ours on this point — it accepts a licence from any state — and we have quoted it beside our own rule in the procedure so you can see the difference. Please tell us as soon as the Washington board issues.
